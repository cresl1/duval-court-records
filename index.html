    About & notices

    Documents accepted into court files are PDF/A as required by the Florida Supreme Court and may include embedded hyperlinks. Treat links in any filed document with the same caution as links on any website.

    Viewing permissions follow the Florida Supreme Court's administrative orders and Security Matrix, based on your role, the case type, and the document. Rules: duvalclerk.com/online-option/court-records

    The Clerk works to keep portal information accurate but makes no warranties as to completeness, accuracy, or timeliness. This app is an unofficial launcher for the official portal.
