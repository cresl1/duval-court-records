⏱Florida deadline calculator

Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.514, with 4th Circuit court and Duval Clerk holidays for 2026–2027.

Pick a trigger date

How it counts (R. 2.514(a)(1)): counting begins on the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday; every day is counted after that; if the last day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday the deadline moves to the next day that is not. Periods under 7 days skip weekends and holidays (R. 2.514(a)(2)). The last day ends 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern for e-filing (R. 2.514(a)(4)). Mail: the current rule adds 5 days only when service was made only by mail (R. 2.514(b), July 1, 2026 rules); e-mail and portal service get no extra days. Rules of Civil Procedure 1.090(a) defers to 2.514. Holidays: 4th Circuit court holidays and Clerk closings (R. 2.514(a)(6)). Court-ordered closures or extensions are not included. Verify every deadline against the current rules, any court order, and the case docket. Rule text: The Florida Bar, court rules.
